Profile

About Me

I am a Computer Science researcher with a background in cybersecurity, software engineering, machine learning, computational intelligence, and metaheuristic optimization.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Aligarh Muslim University, with doctoral research focused on hybrid metaheuristic algorithms for automated software security testing.

My doctoral research investigated search-based approaches for improving software testing effectiveness, including code coverage and path coverage, and resulted in peer-reviewed publications in software engineering, cybersecurity, and computational intelligence.

My current research interests have expanded toward broader applications of artificial intelligence and intelligent computational methods. I am particularly interested in machine learning, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, generative AI, large language models, explainable AI, trustworthy AI, and intelligent human-AI systems.

I enjoy exploring innovative ideas and applying computational techniques to challenging real-world problems. I am interested in research collaborations, postdoctoral opportunities, and interdisciplinary research involving AI, cybersecurity, software engineering, and computational intelligence.
